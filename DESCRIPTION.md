AI drivers have a weekend form, and in **Driver Career** and **My Team** that form follows their season: hot streaks, slumps, steady veterans and rookies who swing. Press **F6** in a session for the **Form Guide**. Built on **msyeett's Driver Form**, with everything it does.

### Weekend form
- Every AI driver is in form, out of form or neutral for the weekend. It lasts through practice, qualifying and the race, even if you close the game between sessions.
- In form: quicker over a lap through the game's own AI pace, hot spells of extra time, harder to pass.
- Out of form: slower, they brake earlier, carry less speed and get on the throttle later, with bad patches and sudden mistakes.
- Your own car is never touched. Time Trial is left alone.

### Momentum
- After every race a driver's streak moves with their result: better than their usual finish and further ahead of their teammate than usual, it goes up; worse, it goes down. A slow car or a faster teammate is no slump by itself.
- A retirement leaves the streak as it is, a sprint counts half, and a streak fades without results to back it.
- Veterans' form swings less than rookies', from the game's experience and awareness ratings.
- Each career remembers its streaks and every driver's last five results.

### Form Guide
- A drawer that reads the grid like a thermal camera: every driver's heat index from -100 to +100 on a thermometer, the time a lap it is worth, the grid's heat as an equaliser, and the hottest and coldest driver.
- In a career, each driver's trend over their last five races as a line, and those results as cells coloured by how hot they were.
- HOT STREAK, SLUMP, FLYING, STRUGGLING, STEADY and ERRATIC tags, and live ON A CHARGE, LOSING TIME and MISTAKE marks during the race.
- It doesn't take the keys from the game: drive with it open. Three sizes.

### Settings
Find these in **ADDON SETTINGS**.
- **Form strength**: Off, Subtle (about 0.8 s a lap either way), Normal (1.5 s), Wild (2.2 s) or Chaos (3.5 s).
- **Momentum**: how much the streak counts in a career. Off is a fresh roll every weekend, like Driver Form.
- **Form Guide key**: F6, F5, F9, F10, F11 or None. **Form Guide size**: Small, Normal or Large.
- **In form / Out of form toasts**, **Bad patches and mistakes**, and how often the race **Messages** show. Everything on screen except the Form Guide and its reminder is off by default.

### Works with
- Silly Season and other career addons. Drivers added by other addons get a form too.
- Not with Driver Form itself: turn one of the two off.
- Team Orders and other addons that change the AI work, and their effects add up.
